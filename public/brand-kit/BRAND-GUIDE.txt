ITO BRAND KIT
=============

LOGO
The Itô wordmark is set in a high-contrast serif. Its circumflex is the
distinctive symbol: keep it visible and use Clay (#C65D3B). The wordmark
should remain Ink (#15140F) on Paper (#F3EFE7) or a transparent background.
Do not outline, skew, recolor, or attach a tagline directly to the logo.

SYMBOL
Use the standalone circumflex as the compact mark, favicon, or avatar.
Keep clear space around it. A useful minimum is the height of the lowercase t.

COLORS
Paper   #F3EFE7
Ink     #15140F
Stone   #8A857A
Clay    #C65D3B

TYPE
Display: Instrument Serif (fallbacks: Iowan Old Style, Baskerville, Georgia)
Body:    DM Sans (fallback: Helvetica Neue, sans-serif)
Labels:  DM Mono (fallback: SFMono-Regular, Consolas, monospace)
